# ristoview app shell

Scope: the whole client app (easter egg, monthly gift, restaurants + reviews, settings, setup). Visitor mode: Operate, with the gift and easter egg as the two authored moments.

Audience/job: she logs dinners on her phone, in the evening, often at the table or on the way home; once a month she opens her gift and picks a cuisine. He sets it up.

Constraints: pink is the main colour (brief-pinned); two real dogs as mascots (Norfolk terrier, brown min pin), appearing at random; Italian copy; nickname from data; only restaurant name required.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Sunday pasticceria tray: the monthly gift is a striped pastry package she unties, and the diary is the shop's counter. Refuses the pastel-card food-diary app with star ratings and a FAB.

OWN-WORLD: Committed pink. Pink-and-white wrapping stripe on the header band and gift box; blush ground; chocolate-brown ink for text and primary actions; raspberry ribbon for the signature move and focus; gold seal for states (chosen, redeemed). Paper-lace doily edge on the gift and dialogs. Faces: Yellowtail script for the shop logotype, gift name and big moments only; Bricolage Grotesque for everything else. Ratings as paw prints. States as printed marks: a rubber stamp "SFRUTTATO", a struck band for expired.

STORY: She sees this month's box waiting, unties the ribbon, picks from the listino, sees her choice on the seal until the stamp lands. Then she finds every dinner as numbered entries on the counter and adds one in under a minute.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: script logotype left, sync dot right, striped band under it. Main: this month's gift box, about 60% of the phone viewport, ribbon across it, a dog peeking from behind; caption with the deadline. Below the fold: "Le ultime cene" list. Bottom tab bar (Regalo, Ristoranti, Aggiungi, Impostazioni) on phone; top tabs on desktop.

FORM: Pasticceria della domenica; my rank 1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK); seed ae3b44c7.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
